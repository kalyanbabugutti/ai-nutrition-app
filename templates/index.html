Nutrition Tips for Everyday

Small changes add up. Use these simple tips to eat better and feel more energized.

Stay Hydrated

Drink plenty of water throughout the day. Aim for 6–8 glasses and include herbal teas or infused water for variety.

Eat More Plants

Fill half your plate with vegetables and fruits. Add legumes, nuts, and whole grains for fiber and nutrients.

Balance Your Plate

Combine protein, healthy fats, and complex carbs at each meal for sustained energy and satisfaction.

Mindful Eating

Eat slowly, chew well, and avoid screens at the table. Tuning in to hunger and fullness helps prevent overeating.

Choose Whole Foods

Prefer whole grains, fresh produce, and minimally processed options over packaged and refined foods when you can.

Plan Ahead

Meal prep and healthy snacks on hand make it easier to stick to good choices when you're busy or tired.
